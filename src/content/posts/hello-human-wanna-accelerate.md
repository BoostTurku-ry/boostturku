Boost Turku is launching Human Accelerator for curious individuals who want to learn new stuff and do something meaningful. Program is open for University students who are interested in developing themselves. Program consists of 8 workshops, other small tasks and couple of networking parties.

Åbo Akademi, University of Turku and Turku University of Applied Sciences will also provide credits for students that complete the program.

Aim of the program is to build relevant work-life skills and help students to discover their skills and potential.

Program:

20.2 Dream Workshop - Setting goals  
27.2 Recognizing your skills  
6.3 Tools and software needed in work life  
13.3 Create your own website  
20.3 Marketing 101  
27.3 Graphic Design  
3.4 Innovation  
10.4 Motivation + Ending party
