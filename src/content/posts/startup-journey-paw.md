Do you know the feeling when you come home after a long day, and at the front door there comes your furry little friend to greet you like you haven’t seen in ages? We do.

![](/images/blog/raapimispuuke-801x1024-0cd5ba.jpg)

*View fullsize*

We love our pets, and they are equal family members in our homes. When we chose to live with them, we didn’t get to choose furnitures that they came with. Or at least markets didn’t have good-looking solutions available.

Påw is design house for pets. We create and make long-lasting and beautiful design products for your pets. Our designs are for you, who love your furry little friend and like to have pretty things around both of you. Scandinavian stylised products are specifically made in eco-friendly manner out of respect to the nature. All textiles are ecological and configurable, which makes them easy to wash or replace if needed. We only use high-quality materials to ensure long-lasting and beautiful products.

![](/images/blog/peti-746x1024-473a93.jpg)

*View fullsize*

We are team of two, Niina & Maria. We share the same mission; beautiful homes with happy pets, and endless love for cats and dogs. Niina has a vivacious, energetic and always ready-to-play kitten Tenho. Marias dog called Kisu rather just lays on a bed watching tv. We wanted to combine our two favourite things; design and pets. Behind that, together we have strong experience of interior design, sales and marketing. Essentially our expertise is managing the whole production.

You no longer have to accept unstylish cat trees or bone prints – now you can have a good-looking home without compromising your pets’ happiness.

Niina & Maria <3
