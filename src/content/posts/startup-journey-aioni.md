Aeon: an indefinite and very long period of time.

Maybe we just picked the name Aioni (aeon in Finnish), because it’s close to top in the alphabet, short and can be pronounced from West to East. Maybe it represents how long we’re planning on staying on the map of game developers. You’ll know it when you meet us.

![](/images/blog/20180726_BBQ_group03-1024-6ab1a5.jpg)

*View fullsize*

We are an up-and-coming game company, with a team that gets things done - in way shorter time periods than aeons. Our development cycles are actually the very opposite that: we stick to our deadlines and get a playable game out in matter of months. It’s all thanks to our small, talented team of individuals: a programmer who debugs during a train ride, technical artist and graphic designer who’ll produce a new look within a given evening and a producer who glues it all together.

Our latest game is Fuzzy Runners, a fast-paced mobile game where you can design the track your opponent must race through. Don’t let the simplistic look fool you, there’s plenty of content packed behind that cute fuzzy face and more to come with each update we’re pushing. The game has three distinct game modes: you can either challenge yourself against maps of different difficulty, play against an online opponent or just try to beat your own high score. Collect the gems on your way to the finish line, so that you can level up your traps to be even more powerful. Or just spend them all to make your own fuzzy avatar the cutest it can be, or the most badass.

You can join our community as well, just check out our [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/aionigames/), [Twitter](https://twitter.com/AioniGames)or [Discord](https://discord.gg/EVNYY56). Or find us on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/user/Aionigames).

We believe in games that you can just pick up and play, but where there’s always something new around the corner: Fuzzy Runners.

- Aioni
