#### So why do we strive to have Head Coaches for each program?

We believe that it is crucial for the success of an early-stage startup to have someone very knowledgeable to follow up on the progress during the whole program. Teemu, Turo, Tero, Jami, Natasha and Eeva have been generously giving their time for the startups and talking with them every week. This way Head Coaches were able to see  **how teams were evolving and guide them through their journey** . Our startups shared with us, that this very hands-on apporoach was very helpful and motivating for them.

It is also important for us to have Business Track Head Coaches who are investors. So, when they are talking with the teams, they have not only the mentor hat on, but also an investor one. They can directly tell to the teams what kind of progress they need to see in order for them to be interested in investing.
