We are pleased to present José Rotiélio Junior, our new intern.  
José is a Brazilian guy who lives in Finland since '17 summer. Art addict, he studied Architecture and Urbanism in Brazil and now is studying BA at Turku University of Applied Sciences.

Creativity is one of his major skills. He joins us in order to take care of our design stuff, digital marketing, and art creation.

*"Work at Boost will be a great opportunity for me to be inside the startup environment in Turku. I am truly excited to show why I am here, help as much as I can and be part of the team. I believe we are going to build a great partnership"*

His life goal is to contribute to a sustainable world, where people can interact with the environment positively without any damage and loss for both.

Outside of work, he likes cooking, plays volleyball, workout, and travel exploring new cultures around the world.
