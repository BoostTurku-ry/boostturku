**Sarah Sahragard**  is a creature who always has lots of questions and love to learn in every aspect of her life.  
She is not scared of taking risk, but she is not sure if she can bare it after it happens :)  
She is the one who organizes  **Startup Marathon 2019**  together with great Boost’s volunteers.

"Roll up your sleeves because we will have a wonderful hackathon event together. Watch out!"

She joined Boost, initially based on her friend’s request to support digital marketing team as a part-time resource, but after a while, she found Boost community a place where she can learn a lot and challenge her skills. She has been enjoying Boost’s different events and she asked herself why she shouldn’t be one of the members.  
She is responsible to manage all tasks related to Startup Marathon and to make it happen with a creative mind to think about innovative ways of implementing the project. She tends to do graphic design and to create content. Also, she is good in event management and able to plan a new project from scratch.

She believes in loving everything without expecting the same in return. She loves being healthy, happy and enjoys living in a lovely planet. She loves animals and exploring cultures and natures.
