Happy new year! We at Boost had a busy autumn, because in November, we organized the first-ever Startup Marathon.

Like our better-known Startup Journey program, the Marathon is a startup accelerator event where individuals get to team up and work together with mentors on their chosen business idea. The big difference is that whereas the Journey takes all summer, the Marathon gets wrapped up in just 42 hours.

Before the actual Marathon weekend, we took a bit of a head start at a pre-event where the marathoners-to-be had the chance to get to know us and each other and start talking about their ideas. We were really happy with how it turned out: after some introductions, we gave the stage to our participants for an Open Mic, and sure enough, we got to hear a number of exciting early ideas over the course of the evening.

The main event was held November 23 through 25 at SparkUp, and we were positively amazed with how much mentoring, workshops and coworking we managed to fit into just one weekend, with amazing speakers: Jere Ketonen, Jukka Vesala, Juuso Salmu, Veli-Pekka Virtanen, Riitta Hyppänen, Mari Jokiranta, Jani Rusi, Christina Forsgård, Sami Sorvali, Henna-Riika Ruotsalainen and Toni Perämäki and sponsors from [Taiste](https://www.taiste.fi/), [Vincit](https://www.vincit.fi/), [Adecco Finland](https://www.adecco.fi/), [Handelsbanken Suomi](https://www.handelsbanken.fi/fi/), [Nordea Suomi](https://www.nordea.fi/) and [SHIFT Business Festival](https://theshift.fi/). We even had the time to squeeze in some all-important networking, and for Saturday night, we transformed the SparkUp upstairs into a secret bar, with our own DJ and bartender and some special beverages from [Nokian Panimo](http://nokianpanimo.fi/).

The winner of the first Startup Marathon, Data Story, was announced on Sunday night, and their idea of finding new ways of supporting management consultancy with data driven resources was awarded with free consulting hours with Nordea and tickets to the next SHIFT, as well as their own table at our office, Boost Academy. If you missed it back in November, you can look back on the Marathon on our [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/boostturku), [Twitter](https://twitter.com/boostturku) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/boostturku) with [#StartupMarathon2018](https://twitter.com/hashtag/startupmarathon2018).
