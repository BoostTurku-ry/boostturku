This week happened to be very interactive. We had 3 days in a row full of keynotes and 1on1 discussions with the coaches. We believe it is very important to have these private discussions, because the mentors that come here are knowledgeable and experienced in so many areas. So when it comes to searching for a feedback, advice or connections, our coaches are most likely to help.

We also wanted to say a couple of words about how truly giving the people in our community are. The coaches dedicate time from their busy schedules to spend it with our teams. And when meeting the Startup Journey participants, they always ask them how can they help. This week these kind people were Marc Dillon - Vice President of ZenRobotics, Teemu Malinen - Founder and CEO of Sofokus, and Tatjana Zavadja - UX and Product Designer.

Thursday's BBQ

Now, enough about coaches. Another highlight of the week is, of course, the BBQ, which was organized by the Evil Corp. It was a very Finnish BBQ compared to last week. We have indulged into classic beer and sausage with a bit of potato salad and corn.

Overall, we are very happy with the progress of Startup Journey teams. Keep doing the excellent job guys!
