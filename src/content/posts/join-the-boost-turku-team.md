All great things are born from people who make them happen. Now we want YOU to join us to make Turku a thriving student entrepreneurship city.

Boost Turku ry's Annual Spring Meeting is coming in April 19th and we have two positions open for ambitious young students who want to make a difference. As a board member, you get to for example organize events, do marketing, write blog posts or meet and network with the key players in the entrepreneurship ecosystem both in Turku and in Finland - the sky is the limit.

The attitude is more important than the skills. We are first and foremost looking for enthusiast people who want to improve things for the community, who want to be part of fast-moving and interesting organization and who have a passion for entrepreneurship and startups.

If you are interested in joining the team, please fill in [this form](https://goo.gl/forms/VtYjhnptbN5abX6o2) and join the Annual Spring Meeting to tell more about yourself. The selection for the board members will be made by the members of Boost Turku ry, for which you can [apply here](https://goo.gl/forms/1duGibDdJcX4AJma2). If you have any questions about Boost, the board or anything in between, please send us an email at board [at] boostturku.com and let's talk!

Boost Turku ry is a non-profit organization (rekisteröity yhdistys) which is funded by our amazing partners Turku Science Park, University of Turku, Åbo Akademi, Turku University of Applied Sciences and Vipuvoimaa EU:lta project. The organization is run by a volunteer-board consisting of students in Turku. In addition to the board, we have two full-time employees helping in running the day-to-day activities.
