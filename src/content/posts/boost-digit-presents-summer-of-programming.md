Interested in learning a new skill? Searching for something new to keep you going? Be you a complete newbie to programming or an expert in your field who needs that extra kick to keep working on your code, Boost Summer of Programming is the place to be. No pre-registration needed: it couldn’t be any easier.

![](/images/blog/programming-2115930_1280-1024x576-4f036d.jpg)

*View fullsize*

**WHEN?**

Boost Turku and Digit ry are organizing five awesome programming events during the summer, starting at Helatorstai 25.5. and ending in 16.8. The events will be weekday evenings at the times that are friendly to both people working and people studying.

**Dates: Thu 25.5., Tue 13.6., Tue 4.7., Tue 25.7., Wed 16.8.**

All events take place 18 - 20.

**WHAT?**

During the two-hour events you can bring your own project to work with, start a new team project or – in case programming is not yet your expertise – learn the basics and start your first own portfolio page with some of our great mentors!

**WHERE?**

All the events will be organized at SparkUp, Tykistökatu 4B.

**For beginners...**

One of the main themes we will focus on will be the basics of front-end development, the content and visuals of web pages. We will start with HTML for creating the content, then modify the appearance with CSS and in the end get a chance to try out using frameworks to make the pages responsive (looking good on different devices) and JavaScript to make them interactive.

**... and for those who already know**

For programmers working on their own projects we provide the social pressure to actually get it done AND the luxury of throwing around ideas and perhaps starting a new project together. Don't kid yourself, you ain't gonna get the project started at home. Come to SparkUp and do it with friends.

**Join the community in our** [**Facebook group**](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1060770640721812/) **.**

We are looking forward to meeting you all there!
