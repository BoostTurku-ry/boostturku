Hi there!

You’ve just stepped into reading  **the story of StepOut**  - the one-stop destination towards fitness & recreation. We have embarked on this journey for about a year now and are extremely excited and determined to see our vision turn into reality.

Our approach has always been to empower people, be it by serving the independent trainers in the field of fitness & recreation with convenient earning opportunities or motivating people to prioritize themselves as well as their health and well-being.

Based in Helsinki,  **StepOut is a digital service that aims to serve the needs of independent trainers anywhere** . We strive to support the trainers looking for opportunities and resources to organize and offer their sessions, receive assistance with marketing and sales processes, connections to studios, access to a free-of-cost live streaming platform, and exposure in various forms to ultimately create a customer base and earn revenue.

The variety of sessions being offered by these trainers will allow people to instantly explore, book, pay, and attend their favorite sessions all in one place without any hassle. Our website [www.stepout.fi](http://www.stepout.fi/) has been serving this purpose till now, however, for even better user experience, we are all set to bring our Android/iOS app to your fingertips in just a few days. The wait is almost over!!!

![](/images/blog/StepOut-team-e5781f.jpg)

Initiated with just an idea, the two co-founders Tarnjit Saini and Sanni Ishfaq have managed to build a diverse team of 8 enthusiastic and talented team members and 2 business advisors. Minh Anh, the Product Designer is responsible for making everything that you see and experience look good and user friendly. Our social media marketing manager Jenina Reinikainen is there to make sure that you do not miss anything happening at StepOut. Trung Nguyen, Nam Nguyen, Zara Kaleskova, and Magomed Futijev form our developers’ team working towards building the product allowing you to step out rather conveniently. Apart from working passionately to deliver our services in the best possible form, our mutual favorite thing is to bond over food. Nutrition coaching is one of the offerings at StepOut as well, don’t worry, not by us 8 but by our certified trainers ;)

For more information about our team members, feel free to visit [https://stepout.fi/company](https://stepout.fi/company)  
  
All of us are equally passionate about  **our mission to create convenient learning and earning opportunities and encourage fitness and well-being for all age groups** . We are highly focused on onboarding quality trainers from various fields. There are no costs for joining the StepOut Trainers Community. It is totally risk-free adding great value to the trainers’ offering. If you are the one or you know someone, please do not shy away from connecting us. We look forward to seeing you follow us on our social media channels! You just never know when you are able to find a stressbuster or a fun activity perfectly suited for you.
